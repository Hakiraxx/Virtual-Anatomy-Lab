# Dental Neuroanatomy & Cranial Nerve Curriculum

## 1. Scope & Objectives
This syllabus provides verified, clinically-accurate neuroanatomical references for dental and maxillofacial surgery students.

## 2. The Trigeminal Nerve (Cranial Nerve V)
The fifth cranial nerve provides general somatic sensory innervation to the face, scalp, oral cavity, teeth, and motor innervation to the muscles of mastication.

```
Cranial Nerve V (Trigeminal)
│
├── Trigeminal Ganglion (Gasserian / Semilunar)
│   │
│   ├── V1: Ophthalmic Nerve (Sensory)
│   │   └── Exits through Superior Orbital Fissure
│   │
│   ├── V2: Maxillary Nerve (Sensory)
│   │   ├── Exits through Foramen Rotundum
│   │   ├── Enters Pterygopalatine Fossa
│   │   ├── Branches: PSA, MSA, ASA Nerves (Superior Dental Plexus)
│   │   └── Terminal branch: Infraorbital Nerve (Infraorbital Foramen)
│   │
│   └── V3: Mandibular Nerve (Sensory + Motor)
│       ├── Exits through Foramen Ovale
│       ├── Anterior Division: Buccal Nerve, Deep Temporal, Masseteric, Pterygoid
│       └── Posterior Division:
│           ├── Auriculotemporal Nerve
│           ├── Lingual Nerve (Sensory to anterior 2/3 tongue & floor of mouth)
│           └── Inferior Alveolar Nerve (IAN)
│               ├── Enters Mandibular Foramen (Guarded by Lingula)
│               ├── Traverses Mandibular Canal (Inferior Dental Plexus)
│               ├── Terminal 1: Incisive Branch (Canines & Incisors)
│               └── Terminal 2: Mental Nerve (Exits Mental Foramen)
```

## 3. Clinical Dental Anesthesia Landmarks
1. **Inferior Alveolar Nerve Block (Spix's Block)**:
   - Target: Pterygomandibular space, superior to the lingula and mandibular foramen.
   - Nerves Anesthetized: IAN, Incisive, Mental, and frequently Lingual nerve.
2. **Mental / Incisive Nerve Block**:
   - Target: Mental foramen, located between mandibular premolar apices.
3. **Gow-Gates Technique**:
   - Target: Lateral neck of the mandibular condyle, below the lateral pterygoid insertion. Anesthetizes entire V3 trunk.
