# Mandibular Wisdom Tooth Surgical Module (R.48 & R.38)

## 1. Overview
Mandibular third molars (teeth 48 and 38) are the most frequently impacted teeth requiring surgical extraction. This module provides authentic 3D spatial relationship analysis between molar roots and the inferior alveolar canal.

## 2. Anatomical Classifications
### Winter's Classification (Angulation relative to second molar):
- **Vertical ($90^\circ$)**: Long axis parallel to second molar.
- **Mesioangular ($45^\circ$)**: Angled anteriorly toward second molar.
- **Horizontal ($0^\circ$)**: Long axis perpendicular to second molar.
- **Distoangular ($135^\circ$)**: Angled posteriorly into mandibular ramus.

### Pell & Gregory Classification:
- **Ramus Relationship**:
  - Class I: Sufficient space between distal of second molar and anterior border of ramus.
  - Class II: Space is less than mesiodistal diameter of third molar crown.
  - Class III: Crown is completely located within the mandibular ramus.
- **Depth Relative to Occlusal Plane**:
  - Position A: Occlusal plane of third molar is level with or above second molar.
  - Position B: Occlusal plane is between occlusal surface and cervical line of second molar.
  - Position C: Occlusal plane is below the cervical line of second molar.

## 3. Surgical Simulation Steps (Real 3D Asset Driven)
1. **Flap Design & Incision**: Ward's or modified triangular mucoperiosteal incision.
2. **Flap Elevation**: Subperiosteal reflection exposing cortical plate and external oblique ridge.
3. **Ostectomy (Bone Removal)**: Round bur removal of buccal and distal guttering bone.
4. **Odontotomy (Tooth Sectioning)**: Fissure bur coronal sectioning at CEJ. Cut plane highlighted.
5. **Tooth Elevation**: Luxation of severed anatomical crown, followed by purchase point elevation of roots.
6. **Debridement & Suture**: Follicle curettage, copious saline irrigation, and 3-0 silk closure.
