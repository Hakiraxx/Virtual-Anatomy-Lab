## 1. Systemic Arterial Tree Breakdown

### A. Aorta & Central Trunks (7 Nodes)
- Root of aorta, aortic sinus, ascending aorta.
- Aortic arch giving rise to brachiocephalic trunk, left common carotid artery, left subclavian artery.
- Descending thoracic aorta giving rise to bronchial, esophageal, and posterior intercostal arteries.
- Abdominal aorta giving rise to celiac trunk, superior mesenteric artery, renal arteries, inferior mesenteric artery, and bifurcating at L4 into common iliac arteries.

### B. Head & Neck Arteries (12 Nodes)
- Common carotid arteries (left & right).
- Internal carotid arteries (cervical, petrous, cavernous, and cerebral parts).
- External carotid arteries & branches: Superior thyroid, lingual, facial, occipital, posterior auricular, ascending pharyngeal, maxillary, superficial temporal.
- Vertebral arteries and basilar artery.

### C. Upper Limb Arteries (46 Nodes)
- Subclavian arteries (internal thoracic, thyrocervical trunk, costocervical trunk).
- Axillary arteries (lateral thoracic, subscapular, circumflex humeral).
- Brachial arteries & deep brachial (profunda brachii).
- Radial and ulnar arteries.
- Deep and superficial palmar arches, princeps pollicis, common and proper palmar digital arteries.

### D. Visceral Arteries (34 Nodes)
- Celiac Trunk: Common hepatic, splenic, left gastric.
- Superior Mesenteric Artery: Inferior pancreaticoduodenal, jejunal, ileal, ileocolic, right colic, middle colic.
- Inferior Mesenteric Artery: Left colic, sigmoid arteries, superior rectal artery.
- Renal Arteries: Anterior and posterior branches, segmental and interlobar renal branches.

### E. Pelvis & Lower Limb Arteries (90 Nodes)
- Common, internal, and external iliac arteries.
- Femoral artery, deep femoral artery (profunda femoris), lateral & medial circumflex femoral.
- Popliteal artery and genicular anastomotic network.
- Anterior tibial artery and dorsalis pedis artery.
- Posterior tibial artery, fibular (peroneal) artery, medial & lateral plantar arteries, plantar arch.

## 2. Systemic Venous Tree Breakdown

### A. Vena Cava & Central Veins (15 Nodes)
- Superior Vena Cava (SVC) formed by confluence of right & left brachiocephalic veins; Azygos vein entry.
- Inferior Vena Cava (IVC): Hepatic veins, renal veins, gonadal veins, lumbar veins, common iliac confluence.

### B. Head & Neck Veins (18 Nodes)
- Internal jugular veins (sigmoid sinus continuation).
- External and anterior jugular veins.
- Dural venous sinuses.

### C. Hepatic Portal Venous System (12 Nodes)
- Hepatic portal vein formed by confluence of splenic vein and superior mesenteric vein.
- Inferior mesenteric vein draining into splenic vein.
- Gastric and cystic veins.

### D. Limb Veins (64 Nodes)
- Deep veins: Brachial, radial, ulnar, femoral, popliteal, tibial, fibular (venae comitantes).
- Superficial veins: Cephalic, basilic, great saphenous vein, small saphenous vein.

---

## 3. Cardiac Angiology & Myocardium (27 Nodes)
- Left Atrium, Right Atrium, Left Ventricle, Right Ventricle.
- Papillary muscles (anterior, posterior, septal).
- Tricuspid, bicuspid (mitral), aortic, and pulmonary semilunar valve leaflets.
- Right Coronary Artery (RCA) & posterior interventricular branch.
- Left Coronary Artery (LCA): Left anterior descending (LAD) and circumflex (LCx).
- Cardiac veins: Great, middle, and small cardiac veins draining into Coronary Sinus.
