## 6. Normalization & Coordinate Alignment

The coordinate space of the assets was normalized to establish an intuitive clinical standard:
- **Origin `(0, 0, 0)`**: Placed at the centroid of the cervical constriction (CEJ).
- **+Y axis**: Superior / Occlusal (points towards cusp summits).
- **-Y axis**: Inferior / Apical (points towards root tips).
- **+Z axis**: Buccal / Vestibular (faces lateral cheek).
- **-Z axis**: Lingual (faces oral cavity / tongue).
- **+X axis**: Mesial (faces anterior dental arch towards Tooth 47/37).
- **-X axis**: Distal (faces posterior ascending ramus).

## 7. Handedness & Bilateral Mirroring Methodology

Per strict medical requirements, Tooth 38 (Quadrant 3, Left) was **NOT** generated by a simple negative scale matrix (`scale.x = -1`), which would invert polygon winding and cause back-face rendering artifacts. Instead, a mathematically sound sagittal reflection was performed:
1. **Geometric Reflection**: `X_{38} = -X_{48}`, with `Y` and `Z` conserved.
2. **Normal Transformation**: `nX_{38} = -nX_{48}`, preserving outward-pointing orientation.
3. **Triangle Winding Inversion**: Face indices `[i0, i1, i2]` transformed to `[i0, i2, i1]`.
This guarantees valid 2-manifold surfaces, accurate bidirectional lighting, and correct dental quadrant handedness.

## 12. Camera Preset System

Four one-click clinical camera inspection presets were integrated onto the 3D stage HUD:
1. **Mặt Nhai (Occlusal View)**: Camera glides directly above the tooth (`Y = 1.385`), providing a top-down view of the occlusal table, cusps, developmental grooves, and marginal ridges.
2. **Phía Má (Buccal View)**: Camera moves laterally (`X = ±0.090`), viewing the buccal height of contour, cervical constriction, and cortical plate.
3. **Phía Lưỡi (Lingual View)**: Camera positions medially (`X = ∓0.005`), inspecting the lingual surface and the vulnerable lingual cortical plate adjacent to the lingual nerve.
4. **Cận Cảnh (Tooth Close-up)**: Anterolateral 45-degree angle focusing on root apices, root bifurcation, and the inferior alveolar neurovascular canal.

All camera transitions utilize smooth cubic easing via `WisdomCameraController`.
